About us

A small team that still tests every car by hand.

Krupa Elevator keeps its range narrow and its team close to the product — most of our senior staff started on the assembly floor.

GWhere we started

Krupa Elevator began as a small workshop serving local residential builders in Ahmedabad. Requests for hospital and goods elevators followed as builders came back for their next project.

1How we build

Cars, control panels, and drive systems are manufactured at our own works, not assembled from third-party kits. That's what lets us stand behind the full unit, not just the parts we chose.

2Where we're headed

We're expanding our service coverage across Gujarat while keeping the same team-to-installation ratio that's let us respond to call-outs within a day.
