The ride

Three floors, three parts of the story

GWho we are

Krupa Elevator is a family-run manufacturer building passenger, hospital, service, and goods elevators. We keep our product range narrow on purpose, so every model gets attention rather than being one of hundreds.

1What we build

Cars, controllers, drives, and safety gear are manufactured and assembled under one roof. We don't outsource the parts that matter for safety and ride quality.

2How we serve

Every installation includes a maintenance plan, and our service team responds to call-outs within 24 hours across our coverage area.
